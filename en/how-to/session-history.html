How it works

Session History and Race Replay

Find any saved session, read its results, replay a race and review your laps.

← Back to How It Works

Sessions save themselves

With Session Tracking on (Settings → SETUP), JMD saves each session when it ends. After a race it tells you where you finished, with a Not correct? link if you need to fix how the race ended. It only asks you when telemetry stops and it genuinely can't tell whether the session finished.

Open Session History from the Home screen, or tap Past on the Pit Wall.

Find a session

The SESSIONS tab lists every saved session. Use the search box, the sort control and the filter chips for league, season, game, formula and session type. The STATS tab gathers your career figures, and the title bar counts your poles, wins and podiums.

Inside a session

Tap a session to open it. The tabs adapt to the session type:

  • HIGHLIGHTS: the story of the session. For a race: results first, penalties and race statistics, then your race: positions, your laps, the position chart, tyre strategy and race events. For practice and qualifying: your pace, valid and invalid laps and consistency.
  • REPLAY: for races and sprints, Race Replay rebuilds the race lap by lap: the running order, gaps, tyres and events. Use the |< < ▶ > >| controls to play, pause and step. Practice and qualifying replay the timing table; Time Trial replays your laps.
  • TRACE: compare laps (see the traces guide).
  • SESSION: the session details and conditions, and where you tag the session with a league and season.
  • SETUP: the setups you used, where you can rename and rate them.

Race results

Race results show the official classification: start position, places gained or lost, race time, best lap, tyre stints, laps and penalties.

Free and Jax Pro

Free

  • Every session is saved; saving is never blocked
  • Every session listed, with stats from all of them
  • Your most recent session in full, with highlights, lap histories, replay and traces

Jax Pro

  • Every earlier session opens in full

Older sessions stay listed with the Jax Pro badge. Nothing is deleted when a subscription ends, and resubscribing opens the same records again.
