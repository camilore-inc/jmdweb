How it works

Prepare for Session

Learn the circuit with About, then build a plan for practice, qualifying or the race with Session.

← Back to How It Works

Choose the game, circuit and car

Open Prepare for Session from the Home screen and tap the circuit at the top. Pick the game first (F1 26, F1 25 or F1 24), then a circuit from that game. The list is alphabetical and searchable, and also finds other names such as “Spielberg” or “COTA”. A small book icon marks the circuits that have a full circuit reference. The car selector only offers supported game and formula combinations.

JMD remembers your choice for next time. As you scroll, the selectors shrink into one line that you can still tap.

About: learn the circuit

About is the default view. Its sections open and close independently:

  • Overview: the circuit map with numbered corners, the key facts and, for 2026 cars, the Straight Mode and Overtake zones.
  • Corners to prepare: the corners that matter most.
  • Setup considerations: what to think about, with a link to your setups for this track.
  • Tyres: what to expect from the tyres here.
  • My Data: what your own sessions at this circuit tell you.

Explore the map

Tap the map to open the full-screen viewer. Pinch to zoom (up to 6×), drag to pan, rotate with two fingers, and double tap to reset. The map can't get lost off screen, whatever you do.

Session: build your plan

Switch to Session and tell JMD what you're about to do: the occasion (a race weekend, a rehearsal, a standalone session or Time Trial), the session, your intent (compete, rehearse, or learn and test) and, where it applies, the length.

JMD builds a structured plan: YOUR PLAN lists the steps in order, and WHERE TO FOCUS follows.

Free and Jax Pro

Free

  • The full-screen map viewer
  • My Data: how many of your sessions JMD holds for the circuit

Jax Pro

  • About: corners to prepare, setup considerations, tyres, pit loss and circuit analysis
