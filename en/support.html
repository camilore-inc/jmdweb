FAQ

Does Jax Max Delta work on console?

Yes. JMD is designed with console racers in mind. Your game needs to send UDP telemetry, and your iPhone needs to be on the same local network as your PlayStation, Xbox or PC.

Which games are supported?

EA SPORTS F1 24, F1 25 and F1 26, using the game's UDP telemetry output.

Is it available on Android?

Not yet. Jax Max Delta currently runs on iPhone.

Is Jax officially affiliated with EA, F1, Codemasters, Fanatec, PlayStation, or Xbox?

No. Jax Max Delta is independent and uses telemetry data from supported games where available.

Is this only a telemetry dashboard?

No. The live dashboard is one part. JMD also helps you prepare for a session, gives you Jax's voice calls and a full pit wall while you drive, and keeps your session history, setups, lap traces and Race Replay afterwards.

Why is there no Race Replay for some sessions?

Race Replay is for races and sprint races that were captured in enough detail. Practice, qualifying and Time Trial sessions have their own review, focused on pace.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Session history, race history, lap traces and setups live in the app's private storage, and none of it is published or shared with other drivers. If iCloud is available, the app can also back up your session history, leagues and race history into your own private iCloud container, which we cannot read; lap traces stay on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

What is included with Free and Jax Pro?

Free includes the full Dashboard, the full timing classification, Pit Wall GAME, ME and SETUP, the track overview in Prepare for Session, every session saved and listed with your statistics, your most recent session in full, every setup listed, Jax's urgent safety calls and lap-line callouts, Racing Tips and iCloud Backup. Jax Pro adds the complete Jax race engineer, LIVE timing, Pit Wall CAR, MAP and TRACE, every earlier session in full, your setups' values and management, and personalised preparation: circuit analysis, My Data and the Session plan.

How do the trial and subscriptions work?

The app describes a 14-day introductory trial on its monthly and annual Jax Pro plans. Eligibility, localized prices and the exact offer shown by Apple can vary; review the Apple purchase sheet before confirming. Apple bills and renews the subscription unless it is cancelled in your Apple Account subscription settings. Purchases can be restored in JMD.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Manage or cancel Jax Pro in your Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewal but does not normally reverse an existing charge. Apple controls App Store billing and refund decisions; use Apple's purchase support for refund requests, or contact support@jaxmaxdelta.com for JMD help.

Where can I download it?

Jax Max Delta is preparing for its App Store release. The download link will appear on this site as soon as it is available.

How do I report feedback as a tester?

Send the device model, game title, console or PC, session type, and what happened to support@jaxmaxdelta.com.

Common issues

  • No UDP data received because telemetry output is disabled in the game.
  • Your phone is not on the same network as the console or PC.
  • Wrong game UDP destination IP, port, or telemetry format.
  • Voice alerts not playing because device volume, silent mode, or alert categories need adjustment.
