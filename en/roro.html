The Roro Engine

The brain behind the scenes of Jax Max Delta. It plans, calculates and decides, learns who you are from every session you store, and helps you go from the basement to the podium.

R2 · In development

The Roro Engine is the focus of the next major release. It has no date yet, and the details below may change as we build and test it on real sessions.

The idea

One brain behind your race engineer

Today, each part of Jax watches the telemetry on its own. The Roro Engine brings it together: one picture of your race, one plan, and one place that decides what deserves your attention.

01

Understand

A running picture of the race: your pace and your rivals', tyre wear and how fast it's dropping, fuel and energy, gaps projected forward, and the weather on its way.

02

Plan

What you're trying to do: stint lengths, the pit window, target lap times, the energy budget, when to attack and when to defend. Updated whenever the race changes.

03

Decide

What's worth saying now, what goes on screen, and what can wait for the next straight. Every call has a reason, and it is dropped the moment it stops being true.

From basement to podium

It knows who you are

Today, Jax Max Delta gives you the information and gets you going. With the Roro Engine, it will know who is driving: your pace, your habits, your strengths and what holds you back, built from every session you store. Like having an AI for your racing.

Your league, your season

If you race in a league, every race you store in Jax Max Delta becomes knowledge: your rivals, the circuits, how your season is going. The Roro Engine uses all of it to help you finish as high as possible in your league.

Racing Tips that fit you

Tips adapted to how you drive and where you lose time, to help you think like a racer and become one, not just read more data.

It grows with you

The more you drive, the better it knows you, and the more its advice is about you rather than drivers in general.

Wherever you start

Starting out or chasing a title, it meets you where you are and helps you take the next step.

What it will add

From the first practice lap to the debrief

The Roro Engine is the groundwork for a set of features that need a real understanding of your race, not just the latest number.

Before

Pre-race planning

  • Strategy options built from your own practice long runs
  • Target lap times for qualifying and the race
  • Who to expect around you, and how fast they are
  • A weather plan, and a fallback if the Safety Car comes out

During

Live race strategy

  • A pit window that shows where you'll rejoin and who you'll be racing
  • A warning before your tyres fall away, not after
  • Threats laps in advance: “car 27 will be with you in four laps”
  • When to switch tyres as the rain arrives
  • Fuel and energy targets, lap by lap

During

Aware of what's around you

  • Faster cars arriving in practice and qualifying, called early
  • Who is fighting whom around you, and what it means for your race
  • Calls that stay quiet once a situation is over

After

Post-race analysis

  • Where the time went: pace, strategy, traffic or incidents
  • What-ifs: what a different pit lap would have changed
  • Practice, qualifying and race as one weekend story

Garage

Setup recommendations

  • Suggested changes to your setup, each with its reason
  • Based on your own setup history and your driving style
  • Takes the track and the forecast weather into account
  • Try it, measure it, then keep it or go back

Practice

What to work on

  • A short plan for each practice session
  • Objectives drawn from the corners where you lose the most time
  • A check afterwards: did it get better?

How it will behave

A calm teammate, not a chatty one

Speaks less, says more

Fewer, better-timed calls. Anything that isn't urgent waits for a straight.

Sure, or quiet

Every estimate carries a confidence. If the Roro Engine isn't sure, Jax doesn't say it.

Explains itself

Every recommendation comes with its reason, so you can judge it for yourself.

One reading of your race

Live, in Race Replay and in Session History, the same race means the same thing.
