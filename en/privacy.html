Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Camilore Inc. handles information for Jax Max Delta, including local telemetry, diagnostics, purchases, support and private iCloud Backup.

Original effective date: August 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who is responsible

Jax Max Delta is a product owned and operated by Camilore Inc. Subject to completion of the applicable transfer documentation, Camilore Inc. assumed ownership and operation on October 1, 2026. In this policy, “Camilore,” “we” and “us” mean Camilore Inc. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. The short version

Jax Max Delta has no user accounts. You may optionally enter a name for Jax to call you; it is stored locally with your app preferences. Your telemetry, lap data, session history, race history and car setups are stored on your device. They are never published to leaderboards and never shared with other drivers. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising; limited third-party processing is described below. Information leaves your device only through crash diagnostics, purchase and subscription processing, support messages or exports you initiate, and — only if your device has iCloud available and iCloud Backup is on — a backup of your session history and race history in your own private iCloud container, which Camilore cannot read. Checking for new Racing Tips contacts GitHub but sends nothing about you (see 4.6). You can delete the data on your device at any time from inside the app, or by deleting the app. iCloud backups are removed separately (see 4.3).

2. What the app receives from your game

Jax Max Delta listens on your local network for the UDP telemetry your F1 game broadcasts (EA F1 24, F1 25 and F1 26). That traffic travels only across your own network, from your console or PC to your phone. It is received by the app and never sent onward by it. It contains race data — lap times, speed, tyre state, positions, the names the game assigns to drivers in your session — and no information about you personally. The app does not transmit telemetry to us or to anyone else.

3. What is stored on your device

The following stay in the app's private storage on your device: session history (tracks, session types, lap and sector times, positions, results, events, penalties, weather); race history for Race Replay (every driver's laps, positions, tyres, pit stops and race events, as broadcast by the game); lap traces (throttle, brake, steering, speed, gear, ERS and DRS samples); car setups captured from your sessions, and your ratings of them; and your app preferences — connection host and port, game selection, language, units, the optional name you ask Jax to use, Jax voice and audio-guidance settings, and the data-retention setting. You control how long the heavier data — lap traces and race history — is kept through Settings → SETUP → Storage → Keep race data for (forever, 1 year, 9, 6, 3 or 1 months, or 1 week). Choosing a shorter period asks before anything is deleted. Session results, laps and positions are not affected by that setting. You can delete individual sessions or all of them at any time.

4.1 Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple and managed for us by RevenueCat, Inc. When you subscribe or restore a purchase, Apple and RevenueCat process the transaction and an anonymous app-installation identifier so the app knows whether your subscription is active. We never receive your payment details — Apple handles payment entirely. See Apple's privacy policy and RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4.2 Crash and stability diagnostics

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and errors so we can fix them. A crash report contains technical information — the error, the code path, device model, operating-system version, app version and an anonymous installation identifier. It does not contain your telemetry, your session history, your setups, or anything that identifies you personally. We do not use this data for advertising and it is not linked to an identity. See Firebase privacy information.

4.3 iCloud backup of your session history

If your device has iCloud enabled and available, the app backs up your session history, league data and race history after each completed session into the app's own private iCloud container (iCloud.com.camilore.jaxmaxdelta) inside your iCloud account. iCloud Backup is on by default; you can turn it off in Settings → SETUP → Session Tracking & iCloud Backup. This is a real, shipped feature and this policy states it plainly. The backup contains your session records, leagues and the race history used for Race Replay (one file per race). It does not contain lap traces (your throttle, brake and steering samples), which remain only on the device that captured them. The race history in iCloud mirrors your device: when a session's race history is deleted on the device — by you, or by the “Keep race data for” setting — its copy is removed from iCloud at the next backup. Session records remain in the dated backup copies until those copies are replaced. The container belongs to your Apple Account and Apple provides the storage — we have no access to it; we cannot read, list or recover your backups. The app keeps the most recent backup plus up to five dated copies, and prunes older ones. Before restoring, the app writes a local safety copy on your device so a restore can be undone, and restoring never overwrites a session already on the device. To remove these backups, delete the app's data from iCloud in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device; it is not the way to remove the iCloud backups. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

4.4 Exports you initiate

In Settings → SETUP → Export & Import you can export your session history, leagues and race history to a single file and share it through the iOS share sheet (Files, AirDrop, Messages, and so on). Lap traces are not included. This happens only when you ask for it, the file goes wherever you send it, and it does not pass through us. You can import such a file on any device; importing never overwrites a session already there.

4.5 Support messages and feature suggestions

If you contact support@jaxmaxdelta.com, Camilore receives only what you choose to send — typically your email address, device model, app version and a description of the issue. We use it to answer you, troubleshoot, keep support records and improve the product. Do not send raw telemetry or sensitive files unless support requests them and you are comfortable sharing them. Feature suggestions sent to the same address are voluntary and are handled like any other support message. Nothing from the app is attached automatically.

4.6 Racing Tips updates

When the app starts, and when you pull down on the Racing Tips list, it asks GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, operated by GitHub, Inc.) whether a new edition of the Racing Tips is available, and downloads it if there is one. These are plain requests for public files: the app sends no identifier, account, cookie or information about you, your device or your sessions. As with any request on the internet, GitHub receives your device's IP address in order to answer it, and handles it under its own privacy statement.

5. What we do not do

No in-app accounts, sign-up or automatic email collection. No leaderboards or public profiles, and no publishing or sharing of your sessions, setups or lap traces with other drivers. No advertising, no ad identifiers, no IDFA, no ad networks. No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because we do not track. No product analytics or behavioural telemetry sent to us. No selling or renting your data. No location collection — the app does not request location access. No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on contract — processing purchase and subscription status so we can give you the features you paid for — and legitimate interests — crash diagnostics, to keep the app from failing on your device, balanced against the fact that the data is technical and not identity-linked. We do not process special categories of data and we do not carry out automated decision-making that produces legal effects.

7. Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete it, either by deleting sessions in the app, letting your retention setting prune lap traces and race history, or deleting the app. iCloud backups are kept in your own container until you delete them; the app itself keeps the latest plus five dated copies, and removes the race history of sessions no longer on your device. Crash reports are retained by Firebase according to Google's published Crashlytics retention periods. Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies. Support emails, including feature suggestions, are kept as support records for as long as they are needed to answer you, follow up and improve the product.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including in Canada or Quebec, applicable privacy law may give you rights concerning access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, data portability or complaints to a privacy regulator. These rights are not absolute and depend on the information and law involved. Most JMD data is under your direct control in the app. For questions or requests involving support records, diagnostics or purchase status, contact support@jaxmaxdelta.com. We may need enough information to verify and respond to a request without creating a user account.

9. Children

Jax Max Delta is rated 4+ in the App Store and is designed for sim racers, but it is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child sent personal information to support, contact us so we can review and address it.

10. International processing

Apple, Google, RevenueCat and GitHub provide services internationally and may process limited purchase, diagnostic or connection information outside your province, state or country under their own terms and privacy practices. Review the linked provider notices for current details; Camilore does not claim a processing location that those providers have not verified.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the last-updated date and publish the revised version at this address. We will not backdate a revision. Material app-facing changes may also be described in release notes.

12. Contact

Camilore Inc. is responsible for Jax Max Delta. Privacy and support inquiries: support@jaxmaxdelta.com. Corporate website: camilore.com.
